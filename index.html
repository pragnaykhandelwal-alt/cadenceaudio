Cadence

your reading voice

An open-source, offline-first text-to-speech reader I'm building for myself. It reads out the PDFs, articles and notes worth hearing — extracted, segmented, synthesized and played back entirely on-device. Nothing about your reading leaves your Mac.

cadence — app source cadenceaudio — this site

How it's built

React 19Vite Reader UI: segmentation engine that re-segments extracted text on the fly, sentence-level skip/seek, karaoke-style word timing.

Node 20Express Local engine server: PDF and article extraction (unpdf, Readability) and a TTS endpoint — the browser never talks to the open internet on your behalf.

kokoro-js Kokoro-82M neural TTS running as ONNX weights (≈90 MB, downloaded once), fully on-device, with custom pitch × tempo × gap shaping per segment.

Web Audio API Playback graph with time-preserving seek, per-sentence pre-buffering, and word-accurate highlighting while it speaks.
